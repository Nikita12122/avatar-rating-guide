Avatar Rating Control · Quick Setup

Ready in 3 steps ✨

Open the app, create a bridge, then connect it in Roblox. Click the third card to open the game.

How to connect

Just follow these three steps.

About 1 minute
Step 01

Open the BAT file

Double-click Start Avatar Rating Studio.bat. Wait for the dashboard to open and keep it running.

Start the app✓
Step 02

Create a Bridge

In the dashboard, click Create Bridge, then copy the bridge URL.

Copy the bridge URL↗
Step 03 · Roblox

Open Roblox & Connect

Open the game, paste your bridge URL into the Bridge URL field, then click Connect.

Open the Roblox game↗
That's it! Keep the desktop app running while you play.
